# Metabolic Engineering

This directory contains computational workflows for investigating **metabolic engineering strategies using genome-scale metabolic models**.

## Contents

- Reaction knockout simulations
- Gene knockout simulations
- Product optimization
- Metabolic pathway analysis
- Flux redistribution analysis
- Comparison of metabolic phenotypes before and after genetic modifications

## Applications

These workflows can be used to investigate genetic interventions aimed at redirecting metabolic flux toward target products while evaluating potential effects on growth and cellular metabolism.
